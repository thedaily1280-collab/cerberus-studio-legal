Privacy Policy — CERBERUS STUDIO

Last updated: 30 October 2026

What this tool is

CERBERUS STUDIO runs entirely on its owner's computer. It has no server, no user accounts, and no other users.

What it collects

Only what the owner gives it: video and image files selected for upload, and the text of prompts or captions typed into it. It does not collect analytics, advertising identifiers, contacts, location or browsing history.

Platform authorisation

When the owner connects TikTok (or YouTube, or Google Drive), the tool stores the OAuth access and refresh tokens issued by that platform. Those tokens are kept in a local file readable only by the owner's operating-system account. They are used solely to upload the owner's own content, and can be revoked at any time from the platform's security settings.

Where data goes

Files are uploaded only to the platform the owner explicitly chooses, over that platform's official API. Nothing is sent to the author of this tool or to any third party. No data is sold or shared.

Retention

Videos and tokens stay on the owner's machine until the owner deletes them. Revoking access at the platform deletes the tool's ability to act, even if a token file remains.

Children

The tool is not directed at children and is not made available to anyone other than its owner.
